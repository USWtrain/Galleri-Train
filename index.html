Module 01
Program Overview
What the Galleri test is, how it works, and who it's for
About GRAIL & the Galleri Test

GRAIL is focused on alleviating the global burden of cancer by using next-generation sequencing, population-scale clinical studies, and state-of-the-art machine learning to screen for many of the deadliest cancer types before symptoms appear.

The Galleri multi-cancer early detection (MCED) test can detect more than 50 types of cancer before symptoms appear — when they can be easier to treat and are potentially curable.

How It Works
1
Blood draw
Two Streck tubes of blood are collected from the participant (10 mL each).
2
DNA detection
The test detects cell-free DNA fragments circulating in the blood.
3
Machine learning analysis
A machine learning algorithm determines whether DNA fragments originate from healthy cells or cancer cells.
4
Cancer signal origin prediction
For the ~1% of patients who receive a Cancer Signal Detected result, the test predicts the most likely tissue of origin.
Who the Test Is For
✅
Recommended for
  • Adults with elevated cancer risk
  • Individuals age 50 or older
🚫
Not recommended for
  • Pregnant individuals
  • Anyone 21 years old or younger
  • Individuals undergoing active cancer treatment

⚠️
Disclaimer
The Galleri test does not detect a signal for all cancers, and not all cancers can be detected in the blood. False positive and false negative results do occur. The test identifies DNA shed by cancer cells and does not predict future genetic risk for cancer.

Roles & Event Types
Who does what at each event format — know your role before you arrive
Three Variables That Change Event to Event
1 · Event Leader
  • Grail-led: Grail Event Manager onsite
  • USW-led: US Wellness runs the full event
  • Provider Office (GGC): at doctor's office for their patients
2 · Kit Type
  • Galleri Kit: specimens packaged individually
  • Bulk Kit: 5 specimens per bulk shipment
3 · TRF Status
  • Completed onsite by physician or delegate
  • Pre-completed, referenced in Clinical Notes
  • Combination of onsite and pre-event
📋
Confirm all three variables with your Field Ops Manager before every event. See event-specific details in the USW GO app.

All Staff Responsibilities
🚫
NEVER discuss diagnosis or treatment
Redirect all clinical questions — this is a collection event, not a consultation.
1

Greet each participant warmly. Direct them to check-in.

Intake
Check Clinical Notes on each patient record for TRF status. Walk-ins: contact Grail rep to confirm attendance and TRF.

Module 03
Specimen Collection & Labeling
Tube draw, kits, labels, and packaging — the core clinical workflow
🚨
Two full tubes — every time
The Galleri test requires two Streck tubes with 10 mL of blood each. Both must be filled to the line. Do not transfer specimen to other tube types.
Blood Draw Technique
1
Arm position
Keep arm in downward position during collection to avoid backflow (preservative in Streck tubes).
2
Tube orientation
Hold tube with stopper in the uppermost position so tube contents do not touch the stopper.
3
Tourniquet
Release tourniquet once blood starts to flow into the tube, or within two minutes of application.
4
Direct draw only
Tubes are designed for direct blood draw only — do not transfer from another collection tube.
5
Second draw
If more than one draw is necessary, the patient must consent to the 2nd draw. Rely on another USW team member for a 2nd draw attempt.
Specimen Labeling — Galleri Kits
4 Labels + 1 Barcode Per Kit

Print on every label: Full name · DOB (mm/dd/yyyy) · Sex at birth · Collection date

LabelGoes on
A1First tube — on top of Streck label
A2Second tube — on top of Streck label
A3GRAIL exit card
A4US Wellness consent form
BarcodeTop of TRF (no patient ID lines on barcode sticker)
Specimen Labeling — Bulk Kits
Handwrite on blank labels (in USW shipment)
  • First name, Last name
  • Date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy)
  • Sex at birth
  • Collection date
  • One label per tube, placed on top of the Streck label
Packaging — Bulk Kits
1
Specimen bag
2 tubes + 1 TRF per participant into 1 specimen bag (3× QC check completed)
2
Foil pouch
5 specimen bags per foil pouch (max)
3
Shipping box
2 foil pouches per GRAIL shipping box
4
Seal
Seal shipping box with purple seal
5
Final box
Combine shipping boxes into one cardboard box for overnight delivery to lab

Shipped to You (US Wellness)

  • Ancillary supplies (gloves, tourniquets, etc.)

  • Exit postcard · Patient brochure
  • Sharpie · Post-its

Everyone signs a consent
Keep the white copy. The participant receives the yellow copy.
You complete the bottom portion

TRF Completion — GGC (Physician Office)
R
Red box (USW fills)
Record the date the patient's sample was collected.
Y
Yellow box (patient fills)
Ensure the patient has completed all fields, including contact information.
G
Green boxes (provider fills)
Provider and billing information — should be pre-filled or completed by ordering provider office. Confirm all required fields are present.
TRF + Sticker Placement (Galleri Kits)
  • A1 & A2 must be used for blood collection tubes — otherwise lab issues will occur
  • A3 and A4 are interchangeable — one on exit card, one on USW consent form
  • Barcode sticker goes on the top right portion of the TRF
  • Galleri sticker is used to seal kits after quality checking
TRF Placement — Bulk Kits
  • Fill out labels accurately and legibly: first/last name, DOB, sex, collection date
  • Two labels per participant — one on each collection tube, placed over the Streck label
  • Labels must cover the Streck label so the window is not obscured
  • If participant requires a TRF: fold the TRF three times and place in the front flap of the biohazard bag

Module 05
Quality Control
Every event. Every specimen. Every time.
🎯
The QC Standard
Quality control is not optional. Perform a full audit on every specimen before packaging at every event.
QC Checklist
  • Completed kits count matches total participant count
  • Every tube is labeled (label covers the Streck label)
  • Every tube is packaged with a matching TRF
  • Every TRF has every required field filled in
  • Wrap boxed kits in paper for safe transport
  • Complete event shipping form and place in box with completed kits
Shipping Instructions
📦 Completed Kits → Lab
  • Label: TO ACCESSIONING, FEDEX PRIORITY OVERNIGHT
  • Drop at FedEx Office Print & Ship Center only
  • If no FedEx Office available, contact Grail event site contact
  • Text FedEx tracking numbers to 301-926-6099
📬 Other Shipments
  • Unused kits + patient exit cards + posters → Grail, Durham NC via FedEx Ground
  • US Wellness supplies, forms, tablets → Gaithersburg, MD via UPS
3× Name Check Rule
1
First check
After labeling — verify First Name, Last Name, DOB, Sex at birth, Collection date on all tubes.
2
Second check
Before placing tubes into the specimen bag — re-verify all details match the TRF.
3
Third check
Before sealing — final verification that all information matches across tubes, TRF, and consent form.

Module 06
Event Execution
Pre-event review, phlebotomy expectations, and post-event closeout
Pre-Event Review (upon arrival, ~30 min before start)
1
Confirm supplies
Review Scheduler. Confirm enough USW blood draw supplies and Galleri GSBs or Kits are on hand.
2
TRF status
Confirm TRF is signed by physician or licensed clinic delegate. Some offices may place orders electronically (less common).
3
Blood collection readiness
Confirm 2 full tubes protocol with team. This is critical — insufficient collection causes cancellations.
4
FedEx labels
Review FedEx labels for completed kits, unused kits, and marketing materials. The correct label must go on the correct box each day. Drop at full FedEx Office Center only.
5
Questions & workflow
Contact USW Field Ops Manager. There may not always be a GRAIL rep onsite — your first contact is always the Field Ops Manager.
Phlebotomy Staff Expectations
🚫 Not allowed at draw stations
  • No cell phone usage when patients are present
  • No food (one water bottle is OK)
  • Do not provide medical advice — ever
✅ Standards
  • Keep patient interactions under 10 minutes
  • Friendly, professional tone at all times
  • Document two-stick failures (no success after 2 attempts)
  • Document 1-tube-only draws on consent form or iPad
Post-Event Closeout Checklist
TRF & Sample QC
  • All TRFs match tubes (First Name, Last Name, DOB, Sex, Collection Date)?
  • All patient information complete, including phone number and preferred email?
  • Physician/licensed clinic delegate signed and dated all TRFs?
  • All blood samples quality-checked and filled to line?
Packing
  • 2 tubes + TRF into 1 specimen bag (fold TRF, place in front pouch)
  • Max 5 specimen bags per foil pouch
  • Max 2 foil pouches per shipping box
Closeout
  • Completed patient count matches appointment list from scheduler
  • Pack completed kits; label box: FedEx Priority Overnight → Accessioning
  • Pack GRAIL materials (clipboards, pamphlets) separately: label Brad Jaciow Corp Printing Resource
  • Text FedEx tracking numbers to 301-926-6099
