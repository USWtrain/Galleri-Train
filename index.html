Module 01
Program Overview
What the Galleri test is, how it works, and who it is for
About GRAIL & the Galleri Test

GRAIL is focused on alleviating the global burden of cancer by using next-generation sequencing, population-scale clinical studies, and state-of-the-art machine learning to screen for many of the deadliest cancer types before symptoms appear.

The Galleri multi-cancer early detection (MCED) test can detect more than 50 types of cancer before symptoms appear — when they can be easier to treat and are potentially curable.

How It Works
1
Blood draw
Two Streck tubes of blood are collected from the participant (10 mL each).
2
DNA detection
The test detects cell-free DNA fragments circulating in the blood.
3
Machine learning analysis
An algorithm determines whether DNA fragments originate from healthy cells or cancer cells.
4
Origin prediction
For the ~1% who receive a Cancer Signal Detected result, the test predicts the most likely tissue of origin.
Who the Test Is For
✅
Recommended for
  • Adults with elevated cancer risk
  • Individuals age 50 or older
🚫
Not recommended for
  • Pregnant individuals
  • Anyone 21 years old or younger
  • Individuals undergoing active cancer treatment

⚠
Disclaimer
The Galleri test does not detect a signal for all cancers. False positive and false negative results do occur. The test identifies DNA shed by cancer cells and does not predict future genetic risk for cancer.

Roles & Event Types
Who does what at each event format — confirm before you arrive
Three Variables That Change Event to Event
1 · Event Leader
  • Grail-led: Grail Event Manager onsite
  • USW-led: US Wellness runs full event
  • Provider Office (GGC): at doctor office
2 · Kit Type
  • Bulk Kit: Tubes shipped in bulk. Completed kits packaged in sets of 5 per box. *Most common
  • Galleri Kit: Tubes shipped in individual kits. Completed kits packaged individually for return shipment.

Confirm all three variables with your Field Ops Manager before every event. See event-specific details in the USW GO app.

Module 03
Specimen Collection & Labeling
Tube draw, kits, labels, and packaging — the core clinical workflow
⚠ Important Tube Label Placement — Do Not Cover LOT or EXP
▼ Place label BELOW LOT & EXP #
✓
Place patient label BELOW the LOT number and Expiration date.
The label must sit on the lower portion of the Streck tube — below the pre-printed LOT and EXP fields.
✗
Do NOT cover the LOT number or Expiration date.
These fields must remain visible on the tube. Covered LOT/EXP can cause the specimen to be rejected by the lab.
🔍
Verify before packaging: after applying the label, confirm both the LOT number and Expiration date are clearly visible above the label.
🚨
Two full tubes — every time
The Galleri test requires two Streck tubes with 10 mL of blood each. Both must be filled to the line. Do not transfer specimen to other tube types.
Blood Draw Technique
1
Arm position
Keep arm in downward position to avoid backflow from the preservative in Streck tubes.
2
Tube orientation
Hold tube with stopper in the uppermost position so contents do not touch the stopper.
3
Tourniquet
Release once blood starts to flow, or within two minutes of application.
4
Direct draw only
Do not transfer from another collection tube.
5
Straight Needles Preferred
Use standard straight needle as primary collection method. Butterfly needles are provided and may be used for harder draws only.
6
Second draw
If more than one draw is necessary, patient must consent. Rely on another USW team member.
Specimen Labeling — Galleri Kits
4 Labels + 1 Barcode Per Kit

Print on every label: Full name · DOB (mm/dd/yyyy) · Sex at birth · Collection date

LabelGoes on
A1First tube — on top of Streck label, but below the LOT number and Expiration Date printed on the Streck label
A2Second tube — on top of Streck label, but below the LOT number and Expiration Date printed on the Streck label
A3GRAIL exit card
A4US Wellness consent form
BarcodeTop right of TRF (no patient ID lines on barcode sticker)
Specimen Labeling — Bulk Kits
⚠️
Name on tube MUST match the TRF exactly
The name you write on the tube label must match the name on the TRF precisely — same spelling, same format. Mismatches can result in specimen rejection by the lab.
Handwrite on blank labels (from USW shipment)
  • First name, Last name
  • Date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy)
  • Sex at birth
  • Collection date
  • One label per tube leaving the LOT number and Expiration date exposed
Bulk Kit Packaging
1
Specimen bag
2 tubes + 1 TRF per participant into 1 specimen bag (3× QC check completed first)
2
Foil pouch
Max 5 specimen bags per foil pouch
3
Shipping box
2 foil pouches per GRAIL shipping box · seal with purple seal
4
Final box
Combine shipping boxes into cardboard box for overnight delivery to lab
Sticker Placement (Galleri Kits — Individual Boxed Specimens Only)
  • A1 & A2 must go on blood collection tubes — otherwise lab issues will occur
  • A3 and A4 are interchangeable — one on exit card, one on USW consent form
  • Barcode sticker goes on the top right portion of the TRF
  • Galleri sticker seals kits after quality checking

Module 04
Documentation
Test Requisition Forms and Data Entry
Test Requisition Form (TRF) Basics
📄
Every patient MUST have a TRF
  • TRFs are either completed at the event on paper forms or are on file with the lab when the patient completes a clinical survey online in advance of the event
  • Paper TRFs are most common at Fire department events
  • Check Clinical Notes for TRF instruction! Some events have a combination of paper and pre-completed online TRFs. In this case, you will find specific direction in the Clinical Notes field in Salesforce — this field pops up when you select the participant’s name from the appointment list. Follow the Clinical Notes precisely when present. Call US Wellness on-call if you are unsure.
  • If Clinical Notes = Confirm Order ID, you must confirm that the participant has an Order ID before proceeding with their specimen collection. Ask them for their Order ID and let them know that their Order ID may be found on the confirmation email received from Grail when they completed their medical history questionnaire online.
  • If the patient requires a paper TRF, fold the completed TRF three times and place in the front flap of the biohazard bag.

Module 05
Quality Control
Every event. Every specimen. Every time.
🎯
The QC Standard
Perform a full audit on every specimen before packaging at every event. QC is not optional.
QC Checklist
  • Completed kits count matches total participant count
  • Every tube is labeled (label covers the Streck label)
  • Every tube is packaged with a matching TRF
  • Every TRF has every required field filled in
  • Wrap boxed kits in paper for safe transport
  • Complete event shipping form and place in box with completed kits
Shipping Instructions
📦 Completed Kits → Lab
  • Label: TO ACCESSIONING, FEDEX PRIORITY OVERNIGHT
  • Drop at FedEx Office Print & Ship Center only
  • Text FedEx tracking to 301-926-6099
📬 Other Shipments
  • Send unused kits and other unused materials from the event back to US Wellness in your leftover event supply box
  • USW supplies, forms, tablets → Gaithersburg, MD via UPS
3× Name Check Rule
1
After labeling
Verify First Name, Last Name, DOB, Sex at birth, Collection date on all tubes.
2
Before bagging
Re-verify all details match the TRF.
3
Before sealing
Final verification: all info matches across tubes, TRF, and consent form.

Module 06
Event Lead Checklist
Pre-event review, phlebotomy expectations, and post-event closeout
Pre-Event Review (upon arrival, ~30 min before start)
1
Check In
Check in on the US Wellness Go app AND call the Grail Representative assigned to this event. US Wellness sends you a text 30 minutes before start time with the Grail rep name and number. These details are also in your US Wellness Go app, event site contact details.
2
TRF status
Confirm TRF is signed by physician or licensed clinic delegate.
3
Blood collection readiness
Confirm 2 full tubes protocol with team. Insufficient collection causes cancellations.
4
FedEx labels
Review FedEx labels for completed kits. The correct label must go on the correct box each day. Send unused kits and other unused materials back to US Wellness in your leftover event supply box.
5
Questions & workflow
Contact USW Field Ops Manager — your first contact when no GRAIL rep is onsite.
Phlebotomy Staff Expectations
🚫 Not allowed at draw stations
  • No cell phone when patients are present
  • No food (one water bottle OK)
  • No medical advice — ever
✅ Standards
  • Keep interactions under 10 minutes
  • Friendly, professional tone
  • Document two-stick failures
  • Document 1-tube-only draws on consent form or iPad
Post-Event Closeout Checklist
TRF & Sample QC
  • All TRFs match tubes (First Name, Last Name, DOB, Sex, Collection Date)?
  • All patient information complete including phone and preferred email?
  • Physician/licensed clinic delegate signed and dated all TRFs?
  • All blood samples quality-checked and filled to line?
Packing
  • 2 tubes + TRF into 1 specimen bag (fold TRF, place in front pouch)
  • Max 5 specimen bags per foil pouch · Max 2 foil pouches per shipping box
Closeout
  • Completed patient count matches appointment list from scheduler
  • Pack completed kits; label: FedEx Priority Overnight → Accessioning
  • Pack GRAIL materials separately: label Brad Jaciow Corp Printing Resource
  • Text FedEx tracking numbers to 301-926-6099
